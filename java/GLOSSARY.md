# Time deposits

Time deposits hold a balance under a plan's interest rules and may have associated withdrawals.

## Language

**Time deposit**:
A deposit holding a balance under a plan's interest rules, with an elapsed duration
in days and any associated withdrawals.

**Plan type**:
The name of the interest plan associated with a time deposit, such as basic,
student, or premium.

**Balance**:
The monetary amount currently held in a time deposit.

**Withdrawal**:
A recorded amount withdrawn from a particular time deposit on a calendar date.
